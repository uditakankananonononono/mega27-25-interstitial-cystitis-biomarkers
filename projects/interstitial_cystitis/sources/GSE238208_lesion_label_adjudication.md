# P25 lesion-versus-background source adjudication, September 27, 2026

The original [iScience 2023 paper](https://www.cell.com/iscience/fulltext/S2589-0042(23)02339-8) reports **25 HIC patients (23 women, two men)**, diagnosed under East Asian clinical guidelines and ESSIC criteria. Its Results describe **one cold-cup biopsy from a Hunner lesion and one from a background non-Hunner lesion area in each HIC patient**. All 13 BCG-cystitis controls are separate patients; their reddish lesions are not Hunner lesions, and were excluded from our frozen paired contrast. The original source's study-level clinical rule and two-site collection support the GSE238208 series design. The [GEO GSE238208](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE238208) series-matrix source saved under the shared core states 25 paired HIC donors and 13 BCG controls, and the committed `results/ic_p25_sample_map.csv` lists 25 lesion/non-lesion sample pairs matched by source measurement tokens. Source file hash is recorded in `results/ic_p25_result.json`. This is **cohort-level adjudication of the site split**, not independent patient-level pathological re-review or a new marker validation. The 31/46 sign result remains a failed registered test (empirical p=0.067893, no panel BH q<.05). No favorable reanalysis is authorized by finding this source.

A [2025 Scientific Reports paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-22053-8) from an overlapping author group uses **21 HIC** participants in a later comparison across HIC, BCG, follicular and bacterial cystitis; its 21-person denominator must not be substituted for the 2023 study's 25-person GSE238208 design. The exact overlap between these publications' patient sets is not resolved in public text and should not be counted as independent validation. Individual adjudication would require linked clinical/cystoscopic or histopathology records per sample; public GEO labels and an aggregate paper cannot prove that for each GSM.

Separate cohort: [GSE28242](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE28242) is an earlier **urine sediment** study whose archived [2011 conference abstract](https://www.ics.org/Abstracts/Publish/105/000561.pdf) describes 5 controls, 5 PBS without Hunner lesion and 3 PBS with Hunner lesion in the 13 deposited GEO GSMs; the published final paper's abstract refers to 5/5/5 participants, so its 15-person full cohort cannot be automatically mapped to 13 GEO specimens. It is not the P25 paired-tissue experiment. Do not merge these denominators or infer paired lesion tissue from urine groups.
