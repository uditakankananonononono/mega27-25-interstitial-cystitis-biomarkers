# Interstitial Cystitis: proposed novelty test, not a result

**Current verdict:** No new named biological discovery, validated new method, or demonstrated same-task advantage. This is an unproven research direction, not a novelty claim or finished project.

## Falsifiable candidate contribution
A bladder/urine marker that discriminates Hunner-lesion disease, lesion-free pain syndrome and inflammatory controls with a measurable noninvasive decision benefit.

## What would establish or refute it
Lock phenotype categories and source-specific participants; compare urine-sediment and bladder-tissue models on independent source-verified participants and the 2025 published marker baseline. Predeclare the endpoint, exclusion rules, analysis code and comparator before examining the held-out outcome; retain null and negative results. If the effect fails, the proposed contribution fails rather than becoming a post-hoc new marker.

## Why present evidence falls short
The existing cross-tissue and paired-lesion tests fail; the used GSE28242 urine series has 13 individually source-verified GSMs, but other source series remain screened. GSE621 old discovery labels include two IC-derived mock-APF samples miscalled normal controls; strict effect-only sensitivity flips 683 of 3,556 signs and downstream panels are not corrected. GSE11783 has 10 IC biopsies nested in five patients, so the old library-level variance is not patient-level inference; a donor-mean sensitivity preserves signs but not independence. A post-audit two-source reanalysis changes all 50 top genes, has no discovery BH q<.05 gene and fails the registered validation sign threshold (43/50, p=.0469), so the old IC positive panel is not a sound novelty claim. Source lead: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE28242. A source lead is not proof of novelty, matching participants, or clinical utility.
